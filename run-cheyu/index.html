About Us

More Than a Running Club

Run Cheyu started as a small group of friends who loved the open road. Today, we are a thriving movement of runners across multiple cities — united by rhythm, grit, and community.

🌅

Weekly Group Runs

Join our sunrise and evening runs every week. All paces welcome — we never leave anyone behind.

🎍

Race Prep & Coaching

Training plans, pace groups, and certified coaches to get you to your first 5K or marathon PR.

🤝

Lifelong Friendships

From post-run chai to weekend trail adventures — our community bonds far beyond the finish line.
